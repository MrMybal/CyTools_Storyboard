Plan a film as timed panels with references, dialogue, cameras and sound. Stage shots in shared 3D sets, generate or import pictures, then export contact sheets, scripts, animatics or MiniMax H3 plans.

## Getting started {#getting-started}

1. Create a storyboard, add sequences and panels, and set their duration and notes.
2. Add character, place and shot references. In Layout, stage a shot and select its camera; in Images, prepare or generate the corresponding picture.
3. Validate the storyboard and export the format you need. Check timing in the animatic before handing a film or workflow to MiniMax H3.
